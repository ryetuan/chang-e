The original myth

The Elixir

In the traditional legend, Chang'e is the wife of the great archer Hou Yi. After Hou Yi receives an elixir that can grant immortality, he entrusts it to Chang'e to keep safe.

When Hou Yi is away, an intruder tries to steal the elixir. To prevent it from falling into his hands, Chang'e drinks it herself. The elixir makes her immortal, but separates her from Hou Yi, carrying her to the Moon.

This adaptation keeps the myth's ideas of love, sacrifice, immortality and separation, but reimagines them through a modern family and a very different kind of Chang'e.
